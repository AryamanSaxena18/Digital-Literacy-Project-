Stay Safe Online – Checklist for College Students (India)

This checklist is useful in finding simple as well as practical steps which college students can use to stay safe from threats of cybercrimes such as phishing, identity theft as well as other online scams.

• You need to use strong as well as unique passwords for all the crucial accounts like email, banking apps. The password must contain a combination of letters, numbers and special characters.

• Always enable two-factor authentication whenever possible because this adds an another layer by requiring another verification step.

• Avoid clicking on links which are suspicious even though if they are received from email, SMS, or even WhatsApp. Always try to verify the information before opening such messages.

• Never try to share any sensitive information such as passwords, OTP's, debit/credit cards with anybody. 

• UPI Safety Tip: Always try to  check the name of the receiver and his/her UPI ID before sending any type of payment through any such app.

• UPI Safety Tip: Do not try to approve any unknown "Collect Payment Requests". This is because fraudsters send such requests to trick the users.

• Always try to prevent your usage from public Wi-Fi for financial transactions. Because such networks are generally not secure.

• Always keep your device updated or even regularly keep on installing the required system updates and only use trusted and reliable anti - virus software.
