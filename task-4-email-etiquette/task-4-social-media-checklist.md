Responsible Use of Social Media for Students of College

As college students, social media will always play a very crucial as well as a clinical role in our communication, networking as well as sharing of ideas. however, it is very crucial for us to understand that the use of these platforms is supposed to be done in a responsible manner while maintaining a positive presence. Therefore, a checklist has been prepared which highlights some crucial aspects of responsibility on social media.

DO’s

• Think before posting: It is very important for you to consider as well as think properly and rationally before making any kind of post to anybody because there is a possibility that whatever you post affects someone in one way or the other. 

• Protect your privacy: In order to protect your data properly and save your sensitive information such as address or even you number use strong passwords.

• Verify information before sharing: Always make sure that whatever information is being posted by you is correct or coming from a trustable or a reliable source. 

• Maintain professionalism: Always try to maintain professionalism as in the future even your professors, or even colleagues might view or take a loot at your social media profile.
 
• Respect others online: Always be gentle or respectful while giving your opinions to anybody on social media.

DON’Ts

• Do not post offensive or inappropriate content: Always check what content you are posting and whether it is vulgar, or harmful to others.
 
• Do not share confidential information: Never post any private details which might affect your close ones such as your friends or even your family members.

• Do not engage in online arguments or harassment: Always try to be gentle during conversation with a person on social media and do not get into a heated argument or do negative interactions with anybody. 

• Do not overshare personal details: Always try to keep your stuff or activities private from you profile.

• Do not spread misinformation: Always give factual news and never try to give unverified information to someone.
